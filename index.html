HCI · ACCESSIBILITY · CARE-CENTERED RESEARCH

Jyoti Rautela.

embodied access · speculative design · care-centered futures

I study what technologies assume about bodies, and what they ignore. My work asks how design can expand human capabilities instead of normalizing particular bodies. It sits at the intersection of HCI, accessibility, epistemology, and care.
